*   **Form:** The Bristol Stool Scale, a clinical classification system for human feces, categorizes stool shapes. Category 3 – "Separate pieces" – resembles logs, twigs, or soft snakes.

Types 6-7 represent diarrhea (large amounts of watery stool or entirely liquid).

### 3. Consistency: Well-Formed but Not Rock Hard
*   **Description:** Healthy feces are typically moderately firm, holding together well enough to pass in one piece or a few connected pieces but soft enough not to be difficult to expel.
